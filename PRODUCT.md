# Product: Richmond Equipment Rental LLC website

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- Homeowners in the Poconos with a yard, driveway, patio or drainage job (source: Facebook caption 2026-10-17, "works with homeowners and contractors").
- Contractors who need help with part of a job: excavation or demo ahead of their install (caption: "need help with part of a job?"; flyer tagline "so your crew can focus on the install").
- Most arrive on a phone from a Facebook link, inside Facebook's in-app browser (inferred from how the business markets: Facebook groups only).

## Purpose
Give the owner, Steve, a real website where there is none, and turn visitors into estimate requests through his existing process: send pictures, the location and a description by text.

## Positioning
A one-man, compact-equipment outfit that takes the dirty work (digging, breaking, hauling, grading) off homeowners' and contractors' hands, including tight-access spots and small jobs.

## Capabilities
- Services (verbatim from caption): trenching and excavation; concrete breaking and removal; driveway and patio tear-outs; yard grading and gravel spreading; shrub removal and property cleanup; tight-access work with compact equipment.
- Estimates by call, text or email; owner asks for pictures, location and a description.

## Constraints
- No logo, no brand colors, no reviews, no prices, no hours, no business Facebook page.
- One real job photo (graded lot with Bobcat E20 mini excavator, Wacker Neuson tracked carrier, laser level), from the Facebook post's preview image.
- Flyer OCR facts (PA HIC #PA230262, NY 2138246-DCWP, Staten Island, extra service list) are unverified: the flyer image could not be fetched, so they stay off the page.
- Static hosting: the estimate form hands off to a pre-filled text message; photos are attached by the visitor in their messaging app.

## Brand commitments
- Owner's voice, first person ("I"), contractor-direct, no fluff (caption tone: "Send me pictures, the location and a description of the work for an estimate.").
- Colors drawn from the trade and the equipment in the photo (construction-fence green, Wacker Neuson yellow, soil browns, laser-level red): inferred, no brand exists.
